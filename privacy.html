Privacy notice

The website enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. This website does not store your form information in a database.

Information you provide

You may enter your name, contact details, reason for contacting the foundation and your message.

Sending your enquiry

When you press the enquiry button, WhatsApp opens with a prepared message. You can review, change or cancel it before sending. WhatsApp processes messages under its own privacy terms.

How the foundation uses messages

If you send the message, Vision Riders Foundation may use the information to respond, discuss programmes, partnership, volunteering or support, and maintain reasonable organisational records. Your details will not be sold.

Your choices

You can choose not to send the prepared message. To ask about information previously sent to the foundation, call +220 385 3607.

This starter privacy notice should be reviewed against the foundation’s actual practices and applicable legal requirements before public launch.
